About us

Hope that grew out of loss

Our founders, Solomon and Meseret, met in Ethiopia and built a family of five, with a daughter, a son and a baby. A single tragic event took all three of their children, Blen, Christian and Emnet.

In their grief, surrounded by friends, family and community, they returned to Ethiopia, where their story began, and started a new one. Through the BCE Foundation for Kids, they offer the future that is no longer possible for their own children to vulnerable and neglected children in Ethiopia.

Our mission

The BCE Foundation for Kids is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit committed to providing access to education for poor, vulnerable and neglected children in Ethiopia. We cultivate lifelong learning in a clean, safe and nurturing environment to help children realize their full potential.

Our vision

A world where every child, regardless of income, has equal access to education, because education is a human right, not a luxury.

Current project

Atse-Fasil public elementary school

Since 2018 we have worked with Atse-Fasil, one of the oldest schools in Ethiopia's Amhara region, on the outskirts of Gondar. It serves 3,600 students from kindergarten to 8th grade.

Families must pay for notebooks, pencils, backpacks and uniforms, and many children spend the school day hungry. Classrooms are crowded and understaffed, the library and laboratory are too small, and the buildings are aging.
